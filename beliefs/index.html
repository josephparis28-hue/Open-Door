Our foundation

What We Believe

Our faith is founded upon Jesus Christ, established in the truth of God’s Word, and guided by the Holy Spirit. The Scriptures remain our final authority for faith and practice.

Grounded in scripture

God’s Word Is Our Standard

The Holy Spirit will lead and guide us in all truth, and the Scriptures remain our final authority for faith and practice. We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God.

We believe the same Spirit who empowered the early Church continues to empower believers today.

The Living Word

The Scriptures remain our standard for faith and practice.

01

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three distinct persons: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, who are equal in divine nature, power, and glory.

Matthew 28:19, John 1:1, 2 Corinthians 13:14

02

The Infallibility of Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God. This is our final standard for life.

2 Timothy 3:16–17, 2 Peter 1:20–21

03

Divine Healing

We believe God has the power to heal and restore us. We believe that God hears us when we ask in faith according to His will. Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.

James 5:14–16, Psalm 147:3, 1 John 5:14, Hebrews 13:8

04

The Gifts of the Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit remains active today, empowering believers with spiritual gifts for the edification of the Church and the advancement of God’s Kingdom.

1 Corinthians 12:4–7, 14:12
