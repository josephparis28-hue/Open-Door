Our foundation

What We Believe

Our faith is rooted in historic biblical truth and empowered by the active work of the Holy Spirit.

Grounded in scripture

God’s Word Is Our Standard

Scripture guides our faith, doctrine, leadership, and everyday decisions.

We believe the same Spirit who empowered the early Church continues to empower believers today.

The Kingdom of Heaven

The Living Word

The Scriptures remain the foundation of our faith.

01

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—equal in power, authority, and glory.

02

The Infallibility of Scripture

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, our final standard for doctrine, life, leadership, and decision-making.

03

Divine Healing

We believe God has the power to heal physically, emotionally, and spiritually according to His will. Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.

04

The Gifts of the Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit remains active today, empowering believers with spiritual gifts for the edification of the Church and the advancement of God’s Kingdom.
